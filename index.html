LOST IN THE STORM

After a violent Arctic storm, a tiny seal pup woke up completely alone. The ice had broken apart. Its family was gone. Its colony had disappeared.

Since that day, PUFF has drifted across the frozen ocean, hoping that somewhere, someone is waiting for him. He isn’t searching for treasure. He isn’t searching for power. He’s only searching for a place to belong.

One day, people noticed the little seal. Not because he was the biggest. But because everyone knows what it feels like to be lost.

❄️ THE JOURNEY HOME

Phase 1: The Drift

The ice breaks. PUFF begins his lonely journey across the Solana ocean. Launch and initial sightings of the tiny pup. 🧊

Phase 2: Discovery

People start to notice the little seal. Socials go live. The message spreads: Everyone knows what it feels like to be lost. 🌊

Phase 3: The Colony

Little by little, strangers become a family. The community unites to give PUFF a place to belong. 💜

Phase 4: Safe Harbor

Every new member brings PUFF one step closer to home. Shared memes, art, and a culture of protecting the pup at all costs. 🏰

Phase 5: Belonging

PUFF is finally home. The Colony stands strong together, proving that family is who you choose to protect. 🦭
